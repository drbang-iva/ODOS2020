| 5 | Macular hole uses the operator-ruled Gass **enum**, not ODOS `graded` notation: Stage I is an impending hole with a yellow spot or ring and persistent vitreofoveal attachment; Stage II is a small full-thickness hole under 400 µm; Stage III is a full-thickness hole at least 400 µm without complete posterior vitreous detachment; Stage IV is a full-thickness hole with complete posterior vitreous detachment.
